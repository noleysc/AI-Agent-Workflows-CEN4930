## Overview
This sheet records how Nolan reads the Bonita Beach flag and tower clock before a dawn patrol. It is a personal knowledge file for go and no-go calls, not an official Lee County bulletin. The flag on the tower sign and the empty tower before 08:00 together decide whether a short session is allowed.

## Flag meanings
Green means low hazard and a normal paddle if the personal clock still clears class. Yellow means medium hazard: stay on the funboard, enter away from the pier rip, and keep takeoffs soft. Red means high hazard and usually a no-go before class because the exit buffer disappears. Double red means the water is closed. A double red ends the session before the car leaves the apartment.

## Tower and solo rules
Lifeguards staff the tower at 08:00. Any entry before that is unguarded. On a yellow flag with no buddy, overhead takeoffs are off the plan until the tower is staffed. Lightning or thunder in the lot cancels the session rather than delaying it, because a 30-minute wait after thunder misses the drive back for a 09:30 class.
