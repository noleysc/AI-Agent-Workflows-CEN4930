## Overview
The 8 foot funboard is the default board for Nolan's pre-class paddles at Bonita Beach access 10. It enters knee-high waves earlier than a shortboard and keeps the takeoff soft when the shoulder is sore. The board stays in the hatch with the spring suit so there is no gear swap in the dark lot. It is not a performance shortboard and it is not a longboard for nose walks. It is the board that turns a short water window into an actual session instead of a scramble.

## When to use
Use the funboard when the forecast or the visual check shows knee-high to waist-high wind swell, when the shoulder note says no overhead takeoffs, or when the planned water time is under 75 minutes. Use it on yellow flag mornings before the tower opens at 08:00. Do not switch to the shortboard just because a cleaner peak appears closer to the pier if the shoulder rule is still active.

## When not to use
Do not take the funboard into a closed beach, a double red flag, or lightning. Do not use it as an excuse to stay out past the 07:35 exit needed for a 09:10 campus parking cutoff. If the rip on the south side of the pier has moved into the funboard peak, move at least 200 yards south rather than fighting the rip on a larger board.
